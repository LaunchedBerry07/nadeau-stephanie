CASE STUDY 06 / CUSTOM GAME DEVELOPMENT · PYTHON

The Sims 4 — Linguist Career Mod

A custom linguist career system translating real-world language and publishing professions into gameplay progression across seven career tracks and 34 job positions.

TechnologyPython · XML · The Sims 4 script and package resources
Project statusPublished mod · Fully tested in-game at publication

The challenge

The goal was to represent the breadth and differences of language-related professions rather than offer a sequence of job titles alone. Each role needed distinct progression requirements, professional tasks and in-game context while fitting into The Sims 4 career and resource systems.

Career and gameplay design

  • Created a shared linguist career path and six specialized branches: publishing and proofreading, lexicography, digital copywriting, advertising copywriting, translation and interpreting.
  • Defined differentiated requirements using combinations of Writing, Logic, Programming and Charisma skills.
  • Created professional tasks including grammar and translation research, fact-checking, indexing, creative-brief drafting and interpreting-exam preparation.
  • Configured schedules, workdays, required emotional states, salaries, bonuses, books and career-related reward traits.
  • Developed custom examination scenarios with success and failure feedback, buffs, events, messages, activities and progression content.
  • Wrote professional descriptions, task text and in-game messages.

Technical implementation

Created a custom Python script that intervenes in the game’s resource-loading process and adds a custom interaction to the in-game phone. The mod uses a .ts4script component containing Python source/compiled bytecode and a .package file containing custom game resources.

Testing and publication

The mod was fully tested in-game by its creator before publication, and in-game testing was a required step in the moderators’ selection process. At the time of publication, the mod was functioning at 100%. A moderator on the publishing platform also gave positive feedback on the level of detail in the career.

Development approach

This self-directed learning project began without prior Python or XML knowledge. The work combined technical investigation with detailed content design and writing, expressing the logic of each professional path through game rules and player-facing text.

A detailed game system can combine technical integration with domain knowledge, structured progression rules and extensive professional writing.

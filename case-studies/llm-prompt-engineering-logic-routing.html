CASE STUDY 02 / AI SYSTEMS · PROMPT ENGINEERING

LLM Prompt Engineering & Logic Routing

A NotebookLM instruction architecture that classifies a character’s relationship category and routes the requested analysis to a task-specific template.

TechnologyNotebookLM · IF/THEN routing · Source-grounded templates
Project statusApplied analytical workflow · Qualitative refinement

The challenge

The literary project Toxique: L’Ossature des Ruines uses a large collection of personal logs and narratives. Different analytical tasks require different document structures, and the appropriate structure depends on the relationship category assigned to the character under analysis.

Decision architecture

  • Stage 1 — Look up the character in a master matrix and identify the project-defined relationship type: Toxique, Sécure or Miroir.
  • Stage 2 — Use that classification to select the corresponding task-specific template or version.
  • Specialized models cover partner and protagonist profiling, strategic analysis reports, relationship timelines, meeting logs, sensorial traceability and narrative transition sheets.

Source-grounded constraints

  • Require exact quotations from supplied source documents when a template requests verbatim evidence.
  • Instruct the model to address every section marked with a specific AI directive and not skip steps.
  • Apply category-specific analytical framing rather than treating all relationship dynamics as interchangeable.
  • Preserve global rules that reject generic, overly accommodating summaries.

Iteration and evaluation

Practical use revealed that some generated documents did not consistently provide the required analytical depth. The relevant template instructions were refined to elicit more substantive outputs and better align them with the project’s documentation standards. No quantified improvement measure or controlled benchmark was supplied.

Scope and boundaries

This is a literary and analytical document workflow using user-defined categories and source materials, not a clinically validated diagnostic system.

Key takeaway

Complex document generation becomes more structured when classification, routing and evidence requirements are defined before generation.

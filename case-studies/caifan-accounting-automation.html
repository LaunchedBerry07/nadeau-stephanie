CASE STUDY 07 / ACCOUNTING SYSTEMS · LOW-CODE AUTOMATION

Caifan — Accounting System Implementation & Operations Automation

Brought bookkeeping in-house from paper records, implemented QuickBooks Online and built connected workflows for employee time tracking, timesheet reporting, payroll preparation and daily sales accounting.

TechnologyQuickBooks Online · Make · Clockify · QR codes · Google Sheets · Gmail · Google Drive
Project statusOperational use for approximately two years

The challenge

The restaurant’s accounting had been handled by an external firm. The information handed over consisted of paper files, with no digital accounting data provided. Employees recorded hours in personal notebooks, making payroll preparation difficult, while daily sales had to be entered into QuickBooks Online.

Accounting system setup

  • Implemented QuickBooks Online from the ground up, using available paper records to rebuild the digital accounting environment.
  • Reconstructed year-to-date accounting history from paper documentation and established the digital workflow needed to bring accounting in-house.
  • Assumed responsibility for recurring accounting tasks, including payroll processing and daily sales entries.

QR-based employee time tracking

  • Designed a workflow using employee-specific QR codes, keychains and a shared Android tablet.
  • Connected scan events to Clockify through Make, using distinct identifiers for clock-in and clock-out actions.
  • Configured conditional routing so each scan triggered the corresponding start- or stop-time action for the relevant employee.
  • Considered exception handling, including a correction procedure for missed clock-ins or clock-outs.

Biweekly time reporting and payroll preparation

  • Used a Google Calendar event to trigger a workflow that retrieved Clockify entries for the preceding 14 days.
  • Transferred structured time data to Google Sheets, including start and end times, durations, entry IDs, projects, rates, clients and billable amounts where available.
  • Included employee review and confirmation before payroll processing.

Daily sales accounting automation

  • Monitored incoming email for daily sales reports from a specified sender and identified the relevant HTML report by filename.
  • Archived the source report in Google Drive and extracted sales dates, net sales, taxes, total sales and tips from the HTML content using pattern matching.
  • Used the extracted figures to create the corresponding journal entry in QuickBooks Online.

Operational use and results

The automation suite was used in the restaurant’s operations for approximately two years. The recurring software cost was about CAD $10 per month; this refers to recurring software costs, not the total cost of development or implementation. The workflows replaced parts of a fragmented manual process with structured time collection, reporting, employee confirmation and automated sales-entry preparation. No quantified time savings or percentage reduction in administrative workload is claimed.

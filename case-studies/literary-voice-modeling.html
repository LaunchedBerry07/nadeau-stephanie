CASE STUDY 01 / AI SYSTEMS · WRITING

Custom AI Skill Development: Literary Voice Modeling

A modular instruction system that models a distinctive authorial voice across formats and registers, while preserving human editorial authority.

TechnologyCustom AI skill · Structured reference files · Prompt design
Project statusIterative system · Human-led editorial approval

The challenge

A recognizable authorial voice depends on register, rhythm, syntax, narrative stance, rhetorical patterns and limits on what may be inferred. The system needed to make these qualitative characteristics reusable without displacing the author’s judgment, especially when working with personal or sensitive material.

System design

  • Built a modular instruction system around a central skill file and specialized reference files for cardinal rules and voice markers.
  • Defined five registers: dissociated clinician, romantic lyricist, self-analyst, public advocate and satirical ventriloquist.
  • Specified anonymity, radical responsibility, anti-extrapolation, analysis of dynamics rather than public blame, and lexical or tonal restrictions.
  • Documented a Facebook production workflow covering register selection, structure, source quotation where appropriate and a self-check protocol.

Iteration and evaluation

The skill is documented as version 3. Tests included Facebook posts and draft chapters for the literary project Toxique. Project notes describe outputs as qualitatively better than generation without the skill, particularly with fewer manual corrections. In one documented example, a post was published after a single generation without further revision. No controlled benchmark or percentage improvement is available.

Human-led editorial governance

The system supports drafting; it does not make the publication decision. The author retains final approval and may override standing stylistic rules whenever a particular text calls for a different approach.

Reported reach

The author reported 46 publications and 980,000 cumulative views in 2026. These figures describe the publishing activity in which the skill is used; they do not establish that the skill caused that reach.

Skills demonstrated

Key takeaway

A reusable voice system depends on explicit register, context, constraints and editorial governance—not a descriptive prompt alone.
